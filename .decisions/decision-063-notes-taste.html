The case against my pick

A taste file can be confidently wrong. If I write "lean heavier by default" after five picks, every proposal tilts that way until you notice, and you may not read the file often. D's scores at least keep updating by themselves, and B adds nothing new to maintain. A only works if rules stay few, carry their evidence, and show up in the proposal ("leaning heavier because of your taste file") so you can catch a bad one in the moment.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • A rule appears only after you say it outright or a pattern repeats (about 3+ times), and every rule shows its source.
  • When a proposal leans on a taste rule, it says so ("heavier, per your taste file"), so a wrong rule gets caught.
  • Notes and taste for your games stay out of the public studio repo unless you opt in.
  • Tripwire: if the taste file grows past about 30 rules, prune or merge. Long rule lists stop being read.

Research context

  • Logged picks do improve later generations, and quickly: Midjourney personalization works from about 40 ratings and is stable around 200, with little gain past about 400.Midjourney docs
  • Preference memory can work without any training: FABRIC steers image generation from liked and disliked examples alone. That suits an 8 GB laptop.FABRIC (arXiv)
  • Pick-based feedback cuts variety within a batch, and seeing AI examples narrowed people's ideas. That's a reason to keep taste visible and to make "show me three" deliberately varied.CHI 2024
  • Figma comments pinned to a layer detach when the layer is cut and pasted, with no way to reattach them. That's why notes here carry the decision-61 address and go stale visibly.Figma forum
