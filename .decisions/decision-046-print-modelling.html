Modelling Helpers for Print

Technical 🔁 Two-way door Medium stakes

Most things people print need at least one of a few tricks: a gap so a lid or peg fits, raised or cut-in text, a hole for a screw, a model split so it fits the 180 mm bed. The studio can already do these by hand with its ops (boxes, cuts, booleans), but it's fiddly to get right every time. You chose one colour, always (decision 45), so parts here are for fit and size, not colour. This decides how many print helpers the studio gets.

Five Everyday Helpers, Tuned to Your Printer

fitNamed gaps: press 0.1 · slide 0.2 · loose 0.35 mm, used as "$fit.slide"
fit-testA small test coupon; print once, tell the studio which slot fit, and the gaps update
textRaised or cut-in text on any face, at least 0.8 mm strokes
holeScrew holes: M3 clearance, self-tap, or heat-set insert sizes
splitCut to fit the bed, with pegs and matching holes
threads, gears, hingesNot included; add later if needed
What it feels like: "make a box with a sliding lid, my name on top, and a hole for an M3 screw" just works, with gaps that fit your A1 mini because you printed the test coupon once. A 220 mm sign gets split in two with pegs, so it fits the bed and lines up.
Add five print helpers as ordinary studio ops:
  • fit gives named gaps (press, slide, loose) that become settings any part can use.
  • fit-test is a small coupon you print once. Tell the studio which slot fit, and the gaps are set for your printer.
  • text makes raised or cut-in text, kept thick enough to print.
  • hole makes screw holes at standard sizes.
  • split cuts anything too big for the bed, with alignment pegs.
Threads, gears and hinges wait until you actually want them.

Works well when

  • Boxes, holders, signs, brackets, organisers
  • Fits that work the first time

Watch out for

  • No mechanical parts yet
  • One test print to calibrate

The case against my pick

For an occasional printer, even five helpers may outrun what you print: if your prints are mostly decorative, B's text-and-split covers it and the fit coupon may never get printed. Gap sizes also depend on filament and settings, so a single calibration can drift if you switch brands, and sources disagree on starting values, which is why A leans on the test print rather than trusting defaults.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • Starting gaps per side: press about 0.1 mm, slide 0.2 mm, loose 0.35 mm (sources disagree; the coupon settles it).
  • Text uses a free, bundled font and keeps strokes at least two nozzle widths (0.8 mm).
  • Tripwire: if you ask for a thread, gear or hinge twice, add that one helper (C, one at a time).
