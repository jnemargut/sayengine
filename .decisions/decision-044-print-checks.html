Print-Ready Checks and Fixes

Technical 🔁 Two-way door High stakes

Before /studio print (decision 43) hands a project to Bambu Studio, the studio can catch what makes a print fail or come out weak on your A1 mini: too big for the 180 mm bed, walls thinner than two lines of a 0.4 nozzle (about 0.8 mm), steep overhangs that need supports, a tiny base that won't stick, details smaller than the nozzle. The studio's game checks already work live while you model (decision 17). This decides when print checks run and whether the studio only warns or also fixes.

The case against my pick

For an occasional hobby print, live checks may be more than you need, and they duplicate what Bambu Studio already does well (orienting and supports). Overhang and support estimates before slicing are rough; Bambu itself says overhang area is hard to judge before slicing, so A's "14 cm² of supports" could be off and nag you for nothing. B plus Bambu's own tools may be enough until a print actually fails.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • Assumes the A1 mini numbers come from your presets: 180 mm cube, 2 walls of 0.42 mm, supports below 30° from horizontal.
  • Assumes the studio's geometry engine (Manifold) already guarantees a sealed shape.
  • Tripwire: if the overhang estimate is regularly far from what Bambu Studio slices, show it as a hint only and let the slicer decide.
