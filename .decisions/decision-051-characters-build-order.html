The case against my pick

This is a lean. Pocket Raleigh and your own game both slip three weeks, and Pocket Raleigh is mostly about towns, looks and traffic, where characters are small in frame. B keeps every date by running the characters as a third build, and the merges, while messier, are manageable because the character system mostly lives in its own module. Tiebreaker: if dates matter more to you than keeping things simple, take B.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • Assumes the character system takes about three weeks: body plans and bodies, motion and faces, imports and recasting.
  • Assumes The Forge Lesson gets recast with new characters in week 11 as the proof.
  • Tripwire: if week 9's first new body plans still look like cousins in the cast check, spend week 10 on style families before motion.
