The case against my pick

Small, frequent asks add up. On a day with ten proposals, three asks each is thirty taps, and quick yes-or-no taps are exactly how review turns into rubber-stamping (the research behind decision 62). B's single milestone session respects your attention more and lets you compare like with like. A only holds up if the cap stays low, asks that taste already answers are dropped, and anything skipped waits quietly instead of nagging.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • Asks are capped at about three per proposal, and any ask the taste file already answers is skipped.
  • Overnight or unattended work never waits on an ask. It proceeds on my best guess, marked as a guess.
  • "Play 60 seconds" asks come at most once per playable change, not per step.
  • Tripwire: if you skip more than about half the asks for a week, there are too many. Fold them into a milestone session (B).
