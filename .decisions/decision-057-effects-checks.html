Checking and Reviewing Effects

Technical 🔁 Two-way door Medium stakes

Effects fail quietly: they eat phone and web performance (the cost is how much screen they cover in see-through layers, not how many particles), they stutter the first time they play on the web, and the same effect can look different in Godot and three.js. Your proof gate is Mac-only (decision 31). This decides what's checked automatically for every effect and how you review one.

What it feels like: every effect gets a review card with Godot and web clips side by side, in place in the scene. Numbers come first: drawing cost against the game profile's budget, a frame-by-frame difference between engines, and an automatic warm-up so it never stutters on first play.
Every effect is checked against the game profile: how much screen it covers in see-through layers (the real cost on phones and web), how many particles are alive at peak, whether a low-end fallback exists, and whether its colours stay inside the current look. The studio renders the same frame in Godot and the web and compares them, flagging differences. Effects are warmed up at load automatically so they don't stutter on first play. The case against my pick

Coverage on the Mac is an estimate of phone cost, not a measurement, and with Mac-only proof (your decision 31) a real phone could still disagree. Parity diffs between engines will flag small, harmless differences until tolerances are tuned, which could nag. For a desktop-first game, B's "watch the clip" may be enough until a phone version matters.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • Budgets live in the game profiles (desktop, web-mobile), next to the existing asset budgets.
  • Overdraw estimated from the effect's screen area times its see-through layers, from the review camera.
  • Tripwire: if parity diffs flag more than one harmless difference per effect, loosen tolerances per effect type.

Research context

  • On phones and the web, see-through effects cost fill rate (screen coverage), not particle count; a sprite twice the size costs four times as much.Snap Lens Studiobugnet
  • On Godot web exports, each effect stutters the first time it plays while its shader compiles; the fix is to show every effect for one frame at load.Godot forum
