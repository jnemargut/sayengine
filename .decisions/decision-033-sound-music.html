Sound and Music From Local Models

Technical 🔁 Two-way door Medium stakes

Terrarium's sound was 2,800 lines of hand-written code plus generated music and 16 recordings. The sound module (decision 26) decides where effects, ambience and music come from, using only local, free tools (your rule), and only ones whose licences let you ship the result. That second part is the catch: most of the well-known sound models (Meta's AudioCraft and MusicGen, MMAudio, AudioLDM2, TangoFlux) are non-commercial only.

What it feels like: /sound "rainy evening on Fayetteville St" layers a synth rain bed, a generated distant-traffic texture and a CC0 thunder roll, and adds a mellow seeded track whose drums fade in when you start running.
Use the right source for each kind of sound. Short game sounds come from a tiny synth driven by settings (crisp, free, identical in both engines). Foley and textures come from Stable Audio Open Small, which makes clips of up to about 11 seconds in seconds on the Mac and is free to ship under $1M a year, or from CC0 libraries. Music is seeded stems that the game layers and switches by mood, played through Godot's interactive music streams and a web version. ACE-Step for full songs joins only after its weights licence is confirmed. Every file carries its licence.

Works well when

  • Lots of small sounds plus calm, adaptive music
  • Everything must be shippable and credited
  • An 8 GB Mac

Watch out for

  • Seeded music can sound samey
  • Three kinds of source to keep tidy

The case against my pick

This is a lean, not a strong pick, because the music half is unproven: seeded stems may sound like background loops, and the only shippable song model that fits on 8 GB (ACE-Step's small model) has no published Mac timings and an unconfirmed weights licence. Three kinds of source means three ways to go wrong and a licence ledger to keep. If music matters most to you, B with ACE-Step is the tiebreaker: take B if you'd rather have real songs than adaptive loops.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • Assumes Stable Audio Open Small runs comfortably on the M2 Air (Arm CPUs make about 10 s of audio in 7–8 s).
  • Assumes your games stay under Stability's $1M revenue cap, or you switch effects to synth and libraries if not.
  • Tripwire: if ACE-Step's weights licence isn't clearly commercial, leave song generation out and keep seeded stems.
  • Tripwire: if you skip or mute the seeded music in playtests, try B's song model for music only.
