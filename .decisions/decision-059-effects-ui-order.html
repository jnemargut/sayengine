The case against my pick

A tiny game built before the game skills (decisions 35–42) won't use your planning layer, so it's ad hoc, the exact way of working those skills were meant to improve. Running it alongside the rig-part framework means two builds again, and Pocket Raleigh moves a third time. If the tool suite itself is what you enjoy building, B keeps the plan steady and you lose nothing but time to a first game.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • The tiny game is scoped to one room and about five minutes of play, using existing characters, motion and cutscenes.
  • You pick its idea (or take a suggestion), and it's built in Godot or the web, whichever you prefer.
  • Tripwire: if the tiny game isn't playable by the end of week 14, cut it down rather than extend it.
  • Tool freeze: no new tool modules are decided or started until the tiny game is shared.
