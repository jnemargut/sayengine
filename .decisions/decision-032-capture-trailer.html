What it feels like: /reel "60 s, gameplay in the first 3 seconds, end on the goose". Claude writes a shot list into a cutscene-style file, the game renders frame by frame (Godot movie mode, or the web at a fixed step), and you review it as a storyboard in the view. "Swap shots 3 and 4" re-cuts in seconds with no re-capture.
A trailer is a cutscene file (decision 24) plus an edit: shots, cameras, scheduled events like "lightning at 0:41", music, captions, and a crop box per shot for each shape. Capture is frame by frame from the game itself: Godot's movie mode at a fixed frame rate, and the web build's fixed-step mode encoded in the browser. The daemon cuts on the music's beats and writes the final edit as an ffmpeg recipe, so a re-cut never needs a re-capture. Templates follow trailer craft: gameplay in the first seconds, a hook by 10–15 s, 60–90 s long.

Works well when

  • You re-cut often (Terrarium did five times)
  • One capture should feed every shape
  • Events must happen on cue

Watch out for

  • Text and titles are plain compared with a motion-graphics tool
  • Depends on weeks 7–8 landing first

The case against my pick

Good trailers are an editor's craft, and a beat grid plus a template can produce something tidy but forgettable; Terrarium's recuts were about story and pacing, not timing. A's titles and text will be plain next to Motion Canvas, and an explainer video is mostly text and diagrams. It also can't start until the cutscene players from weeks 7–8 exist, while C or D could be used next week.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • Assumes the cutscene format from weeks 7–8 can carry an edit track (cuts, crops, captions, music) without bending out of shape.
  • Assumes beat detection runs as a separate local tool (librosa, permissive licence), never shipped inside a game.
  • Tripwire: if you rewrite titles or text by hand in more than one trailer, add Motion Canvas just for title cards.

Research context

  • Godot's movie mode (--write-movie + --fixed-fps) renders frame by frame with audio in sync, even in exported builds.Godot docs
  • For the web, mp4-muxer is deprecated; its successor Mediabunny encodes in the browser with WebCodecs.mp4-muxer
  • Screen-recording a browser runs in real time and drops frames; a fixed-step mode like Terrarium's ?capture=1 is the robust route.Overcrank
  • MoviePy 2 broke its API and most examples online are v1; generating ffmpeg recipes directly is simpler.MoviePy
  • Remotion is free for individuals and teams up to 3, paid at 4+; Motion Canvas is MIT.Remotion licence
  • auto-editor cuts on loudness or motion, not on beats.auto-editor
  • For vertical video, keep key action and captions inside a centred 900×1400 box on a 1080×1920 frame.Safe zones
