The case against my pick

A demo isn't a game. Pocket Raleigh proves the modules fit but ships nothing, and thin modules can hide how hard the deep versions are (a three-block town says little about a whole city's data and performance). If you already know the next game you want to make, C will teach the suite more, because a real game pushes harder than a demo. And six weeks after week 8 is a long stretch before anything is shareable beyond your own screen.

Assumptions and tripwires

  • Assumes weeks 5–8 land on time; this plan starts after "The Forge Lesson".
  • Assumes three blocks (Fayetteville St near the Capitol, or Glenwood South) have Overture heights and roof shapes.
  • Tripwire: if week 9's buildings look generic, spend week 10 on per-region style rules before adding the invented source.
  • Tripwire: if a game idea takes hold during weeks 9–14, switch to C at the next week boundary and keep what's built.
