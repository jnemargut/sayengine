# Design decision catalogue

How to spread the four options for each design decision, and what each card shows.

## Camera + controls + character (step 3)

Combine them; don't split. Spread across distance and agency:

- close follow behind the character (intimate, harder to plan routes),
- high follow or three-quarter top-down (readable, good for routes and puzzles),
- fixed or room camera (cinematic, simple controls),
- free orbit (exploration, more controls to learn).

Each option names the controls in plain words (WASD to push, Space to brake) and the character's body plan if the studio has a character for it. Compare: readability of the space · how the verb feels · phone friendliness · motion sickness risk · undo cost.

Visual: playable presets of the current toy (iframe per card, `?preset=A`…).

## Feel (step 3)

Name the feel in human words, then the numbers behind it. Typical spread for a moving verb: heavy, floaty, snappy, drifty. For a throwing verb: lobbed, flat, spinning, magnetic. Mention the juice each preset carries (dust puffs, a small screen shake, squash).

Compare: how it feels in the first ten seconds · how it feels after five minutes · skill ceiling · fits the pillars · undo cost.

Visual: playable presets.

## Juice (optional, step 3)

Four bundles of small reactions in order of impact: sound + squash; dust + shake; slow-motion on success; particles and screen flash. Visual: 4-second clips or presets.

## The loop (step 4)

Loop-led: four loops of three to five steps, each with the verb at the centre, a lap time, and what grows each lap. Toy-led: four reasons to come back (collect, decorate, discover, share). Story-led: four opening scenes.

Compare: lap time · what grows · fits the pillars · build effort for a grey box · undo cost.

Visual: a loop diagram (circle of steps, verb in the middle) per option.

## The look (step 6)

Four looks that change mood, not just hue: for example warm toy, crisp daylight, dusk with long shadows, flat poster. Each card is a render of the actual scene in that look, with a palette strip and, when the look lab exists, its colourfulness and palette numbers.

Compare: readability of the verb · mood · fits the pillars · phone cost · undo cost.

## World (step 6)

Real, invented, remix, or a single handcrafted room. Size in streets, rooms or minutes. Visual: top-down map renders or sketches.

## Cast (step 6)

Four casting options, each a small set of characters (body plan, face style, role). Visual: the studio's cast lineup render; flag look-alikes the cast check would catch.

## Sound and music direction (optional, step 6)

Four directions described in a sentence each plus the instruments or textures; clips from the studio's sound module when present. Respect the user's local-and-free rule for any generated audio.
